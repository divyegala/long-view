Detailed comparison & model context

Repeated power is the mean of the strongest three non-overlapping blocks. They can be parts of the same long effort, not necessarily three intentional repetitions. Fatigue indices use nonlinear effort cost and separate fast/slow recovery, with zero initial within-ride load. They are experimental and do not measure your physiological fatigue directly.

Experimental fatigue parameters

The aerobic component uses a shared absolute-power reference. Extra cost rises near 80% of the environment’s threshold, then increases nonlinearly. This proposed model is not a fitted physiological measurement.

All elapsed recording gaps are stops. Recovery is automatic; stops contribute zero power to elapsed-duration efforts. No confirmation is needed. This app never changes your Intervals training plan or settings.
